# Wedding starter provenance
Adapted from elix-stack/wedding-invitation-1, commit 1ac23948c42febb1150d4ddf6d10a39211471448 (MIT).
https://github.com/elix-stack/wedding-invitation-1

Retains the upstream invitation flow and section identifiers (hero, home, info, story, rsvp), invitation anchor and query-based guest personalization pattern. Markup, copy and styles substantially rewritten. Countdown and RSVP scripts replaced with timezone-explicit, dependency-free modules. Added calendar export and local RSVP preview. Removed scroll locking, autoplay music, Bootstrap/lightbox/countdown dependencies, original personal imagery, gift/bank data and remote form destination. No responses are sent to the upstream author. LICENSE-UPSTREAM is reproduced verbatim, including its original copyright placeholder.

Also evaluated dewanakl/undangan (MIT); chose this simpler static base to avoid its broader service setup.

Visual revision: the live hero uses generated garden photography; the former garden.svg remains as an optional illustrative asset. The new visual design uses Cormorant Garamond with Montserrat, and the contact flow remains a local RSVP draft.
